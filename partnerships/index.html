PARTNERSHIP QUESTIONS

A few things to know.

Can you deliver under our agency’s brand?

Yes. I can discuss white-label delivery, how client communication is handled, and any confidentiality requirements before agreeing to a project.

Can we start with a small project?

Absolutely. A defined first project or milestone is a practical way to understand how we work together before planning a longer engagement.

How do pricing and availability work?

I scope pricing and availability around the project and collaboration model. Share the opportunity, expected timeline, and required involvement so we can discuss a realistic plan.

Who owns the code and deliverables?

Ownership, access, handover, and any third-party licensing are defined in the project agreement. I clarify those details with you before work starts.
