BEFORE GETTING STARTED

A little more clarity.

How much does a project cost?

Every project starts with a conversation about scope, complexity, and the outcome you need. I then propose a defined approach and estimate, so you can make an informed decision before work begins.

Can you work with our existing website or application?

Yes. I can review your current setup, understand its constraints, and discuss improvements, integrations, or a phased rebuild. We’ll agree on the scope before making changes.

What happens after launch?

I plan the handover and discuss any ongoing maintenance or development needs as part of the project. Continued support can be scoped separately around your priorities.

Do you work with agencies and other businesses?

Yes. I work with agencies and other businesses on white-label project delivery and ongoing development. Explore the partnerships page for ways to work together.
